My project for my Capstone class.

Survive the onslaught of enemies and upgrade your abilities to get the best score or survival time you can!

Move with WASD
Basic spell: left click
Special spell: right click, but this uses more mana.
Dash with space

Your spells charge as you shoot and will shoot stronger versions of themselves when used enough. 

Your shield and knife cooldowns can be found in the top left, make sure to not underutilize them!

Pressing (R) will end your run for you.

Thank you for playing!
